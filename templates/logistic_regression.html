Sigmoid Mapping Rationale:
Logistic Regression maps feature dot products to bounded probabilities \( P(y=1|X) \in (0, 1) \). Because gradient descent in logistic regression relies on feature scale homogeneity, feature scaling (StandardScaler vs RobustScaler) is essential for rapid weight convergence.
