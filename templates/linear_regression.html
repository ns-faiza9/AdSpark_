Linear Regression Limit on Binary Target:
Linear regression models continuous outcomes \( y \in (-\infty, \infty) \). When applied to binary CTR targets \( y \in \{0, 1\} \), linear models yield unconstrained predictions (sometimes negative or > 1) and suffer from low \( R^2 \) ({{ summary.r2 | default('0.0422') | round(4) }}) due to strong non-linear feature interactions.
