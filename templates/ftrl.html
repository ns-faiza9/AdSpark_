📐 Mathematical Formulation & Coordinate Update Rule

Standard stochastic gradient descent with $L_1$ penalty fails to produce exact zeros due to gradient oscillations around the non-differentiable origin. Google's Follow-The-Regularized-Leader (FTRL-Proximal) solves this by accumulating past gradients $z_{t, i}$ and dynamically soft-thresholding weights:

Coordinate-wise Weight Update ($w_{t+1, i}$):

$$w_{t+1, i} = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } |z_{t, i}| \le \lambda_1 \\ -\text{sgn}(z_{t, i}) \cdot \dfrac{|z_{t, i}| - \lambda_1}{\left(\dfrac{\beta + \sqrt{n_{t, i}}}{\alpha}\right) + \lambda_2} & \text{if } |z_{t, i}| > \lambda_1 \end{cases}$$
Accumulation & Learning Rate Adaptation:

$$\sigma_{t, i} = \frac{\sqrt{n_{t, i} + g_{t, i}^2} - \sqrt{n_{t, i}}}{\alpha}, \quad z_{t+1, i} = z_{t, i} + g_{t, i} - \sigma_{t, i} w_{t, i}, \quad n_{t+1, i} = n_{t, i} + g_{t, i}^2$$
