📐 Mathematical Proof: The $\mathcal{O}(k \cdot d)$ Linear-Time Identity

The 2nd-order Factorization Machine models all pairwise interactions between feature coordinates using factorized dot products $\langle \mathbf{v}_i, \mathbf{v}_j \rangle = \sum_{f=1}^k v_{i, f} v_{j, f}$:

FM Predictive Equation:

$$\hat{y}(\mathbf{x}) = w_0 + \sum_{i=1}^d w_i x_i + \sum_{i=1}^d \sum_{j=i+1}^d \langle \mathbf{v}_i, \mathbf{v}_j \rangle x_i x_j$$
Algebraic Identity Derivation (Proof of Linear Time):

$$\begin{aligned} \sum_{i=1}^d \sum_{j=i+1}^d \langle \mathbf{v}_i, \mathbf{v}_j \rangle x_i x_j &= \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i=1}^d \sum_{j=1}^d \langle \mathbf{v}_i, \mathbf{v}_j \rangle x_i x_j - \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i=1}^d \langle \mathbf{v}_i, \mathbf{v}_i \rangle x_i^2 \\ &= \frac{1}{2} \sum_{f=1}^k \left[ \left( \sum_{i=1}^d v_{i, f} x_i \right)^2 - \sum_{i=1}^d v_{i, f}^2 x_i^2 \right] \end{aligned}$$ This collapses quadratic nested loops into single matrix-vector multiplications requiring strictly $\mathcal{O}(k \cdot d)$ operations!
