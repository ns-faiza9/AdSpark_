📐 Mathematical Formulation: Beta-Binomial Conjugate Prior & WoE / IV

1. Empirical Bayes Smoothing Formula:

$$\text{Smoothed CTR}_i = \frac{C_i + \alpha}{N_i + \alpha + \beta} = \frac{C_i + \mu \cdot m}{N_i + m}$$ Where $C_i$ is click count, $N_i$ is impression count, $\mu$ is the population mean prior CTR, and $m$ is the effective prior pseudo-count weight.
2. Weight of Evidence (WoE) & Information Value (IV):

$$\text{WoE}_i = \ln \left( \frac{\% \text{ Clicks}_i}{\% \text{ Non-Clicks}_i} \right) = \ln \left( \frac{C_i / C_{\text{total}}}{(N_i - C_i) / (N_{\text{total}} - C_{\text{total}})} \right)$$ $$\text{IV} = \sum_{i} \left( \frac{C_i}{C_{\text{total}}} - \frac{N_i - C_i}{N_{\text{total}} - C_{\text{total}}} \right) \times \text{WoE}_i$$
